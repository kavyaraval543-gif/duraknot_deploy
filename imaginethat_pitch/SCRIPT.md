# Pulse: #ImagineThat Idea Pitch, script for each slide (about 2:00)

Speak calmly. Look at the camera, not the slides. The time next to each slide is a target.
**Bold** words are the ones to stress.

---

### Slide 1: Title (0:00–0:08)
> Hi, I'm Kavya Raval. My idea is called **Pulse**: a plug-in brain for every small factory machine in India.

### Slide 2: The question (0:08–0:22)
> Walk into almost any small factory and ask one simple question: *how much did this machine produce today, and why did it stop?*
> Most owners **can't answer**. It's not that they don't care. The data **simply doesn't exist**.

### Slide 3: The problem (0:22–0:42)
> India has more than **sixty million** MSMEs, and most of them still run on paper registers and guesswork.
> Their machines quietly lose hours to **idle time**, to small **two-minute stoppages** that add up, and to **defects** that nobody notices until dispatch.
> Industrial monitoring software does exist, but it's priced for big plants. Small factories are left out.

### Slide 4: The idea (0:42–1:00)
> Pulse is a **low-cost sensor kit** that clips onto a machine the factory already owns. There's **no rewiring and no new machinery**.
> A rotary encoder counts output and speed, a vibration sensor flags defect patterns, and a small ESP32 module streams the data every second.
> Our target is for the kit to cost **less than a smartphone**.

### Slide 5: How it works (1:00–1:18)
> The data goes straight to a simple dashboard on the owner's phone.
> Pulse calculates **OEE**, the world-standard score for how well a machine is really being used.
> It also sends alerts in plain language, like *"Line 2 has been idle for 9 minutes."* The owner can act right away instead of finding out at the end of the day.

### Slide 6: Already built (1:18–1:36)
> This isn't just a slide. My teammate and I have already **built a working prototype** for a real wire-fence manufacturer, Duraknot.
> It has ESP32 firmware, a live backend with an alert engine, and a dashboard deployed online. The whole thing is tested and calibrated against industry benchmarks.

### Slide 7: Why it matters (1:36–1:52)
> Why does this matter? Even a **five percent** gain in utilisation means more output from machines a factory already owns.
> That means **more income** for owners, **more jobs** as small units grow, and **less wasted** material and energy.
> Multiply that across millions of MSMEs, and you strengthen the backbone of India's economy.

### Slide 8: The vision (1:52–2:00)
> Pulse gives the smallest factory the same intelligence as the biggest one.
> Because **when every machine has a pulse, every maker can grow**. Thank you.
